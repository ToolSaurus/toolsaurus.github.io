RingSizer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

RingSizer is an on-screen ring measurement and ring size conversion utility developed by ToolSaurus. This policy describes the data practices of the current version of the Android app.

1. Personal Information

RingSizer does not require an account or ask you to provide personal information. The current app does not transmit your ring measurements or calibration settings to ToolSaurus servers.

2. Measurements and Calibration

Ring measurements and size conversions are calculated on the device. Screen calibration settings are saved locally so that the app can use them when you return. You can reset calibration within the app.

3. Backup and Device Transfer

The app is configured to exclude its stored app data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, because screen calibration is device-specific. Android backup and transfer behavior can vary by operating system version and device manufacturer.

4. Internet, Ads, and Analytics

The current version works offline and does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking SDKs.

5. Sharing of Data

ToolSaurus does not collect ring measurement or calibration data through the app and does not sell or share such data.

6. Children's Privacy

RingSizer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

If the app's functionality or data practices change, this page may be updated to reflect the new behavior.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact ToolSaurus using the developer contact information on its Google Play listing.
