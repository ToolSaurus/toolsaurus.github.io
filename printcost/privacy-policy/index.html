PrintCost Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

PrintCost is a 3D printing cost calculator developed by ToolSaurus. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles user data.

1. Data Collection

PrintCost does not collect personal information or transmit user-entered calculation data to ToolSaurus or third-party servers.

2. Calculation Data

Information entered into the calculator, such as material costs, electricity rates, print duration, and pricing values, is processed locally on your device to produce calculation results.

3. Internet and Third-Party Services

The current version of PrintCost works offline and does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, or user-tracking SDKs.

4. Data Sharing

ToolSaurus does not sell or share personal data collected through PrintCost because the current version does not collect such data.

5. Children's Privacy

PrintCost does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact ToolSaurus through the developer contact information provided on its Google Play Store listing.
